Sky atlas data credits

Geographic time-zone boundaries: timezone-boundary-builder, derived from © OpenStreetMap contributors, distributed under the Open Database License. Comprehensive boundaries are provided by geo-tz 8.1.8, split into byte-identical pieces for local loading. The transformation script and dependency lockfile are in the source repository.

Timekeeping rules come from the browser’s IANA time-zone database. No location lookup is sent to an external service.

Constellation regions are the IAU boundaries as tabulated by Nancy G. Roman (1987, CDS VI/42), in the 1875 equinox they were drawn in; the precession from J2000 was generated, and the lookup independently checked, with Astropy. The meanings of the names and the chart links are from the IAU constellation guide.
